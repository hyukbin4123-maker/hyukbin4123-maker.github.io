Privacy Policy

Bean Vault Sync · Effective 2026-10-26

What data is accessed

Bean Vault Sync accesses files in the Google Drive account of its single user, only to upload, download, and update that user's own Obsidian vault files.

How data is used

Data is used solely to synchronize the user's notes between the user's own devices. No data is collected, stored on any other server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Use of Google user data

Bean Vault Sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
